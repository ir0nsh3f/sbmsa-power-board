Experimental 5U Girls goal counts

Enormous uncertainty with one or two games. Not betting odds, shot-based xG, or calibrated prediction. This dedicated model is separate from both opponent-adjusted power rankings and flag projections. No sportsbook connections or player data.

Scope and mean goals

Fall 2026 5U Girls only: Akers and Boxx. Let μ be total scored goals divided by twice the number of verified completed games with known kickoff before collection. At least four pooled games and a positive mean are required; otherwise forecasts are withheld. Pooling assumes a shared scoring environment, not measured equal division strength.

For team i with n games, goals for GF and goals against GA, use Gamma–Poisson posterior means: attack aᵢ = (GFᵢ + 3μ)/(nᵢ + 3); concession dᵢ = (GAᵢ + 3μ)/(nᵢ + 3). Expected home goals mₕ = (aₕ + dₐ)/2; expected away goals mₐ = (aₐ + dₕ)/2. Thus three pooled-prior games shrink each component; a zero-game team starts at μ. No home advantage or strength-of-opponents adjustment is estimated. Early opponents and disconnected divisions may distort these descriptive inputs.

Broad count uncertainty

Independent negative-binomial goal distributions approximate Gamma-mixed Poisson counts. For each side, the Gamma rate variance is the sum of: one quarter of the two component posterior variances (GF + 3μ)/(n + 3)² or (GA + 3μ)/(n + 3)²; baseline uncertainty max(μ + μ²/2, empirical population variance of team goals)/(2 × pooled games); and an additional m²/2 dispersion allowance. The matched Gamma shape is m²/rate variance. Count variance is m + rate variance.

Exact discrete convolution of these two distributions gives home-margin and total distributions; show the 2.5th and 97.5th percentile endpoints. Computation truncates each count tail only after accumulated mass exceeds 1 − 10⁻¹². These approximate 95% model-based ranges are conditional on untested independence, shared-baseline and dispersion assumptions—not demonstrated 95% coverage. Shared-baseline covariance and team-development effects are not modeled. Prior weight three and extra dispersion shape two are conservative editorial assumptions, not fitted optimal values.

Displayed line and timing

Home margin = mₕ − mₐ; total = mₕ + mₐ. The named favorite always has a minus sign; round absolute expected margin and combined total independently to the nearest half goal, with half-step ties rounded up. Rounded-zero margin is Pick’em. This summarizes the mean margin, not the handicap producing equal cover probabilities.

The team tab selects the next two actual dated, wholly unplayed Rainbow Unicorns fixtures in Boxx, strictly more than 30 minutes after the current instant. Forecast training ends at the actual accepted source collection; forecast kickoff must also be more than 30 minutes after that collection. Unknown kickoff, partial scores and already-started fixtures receive no new projection.

Immutable evidence

Captures and publication receipts are append-only in a separate soccer archive; unchanged model outputs reuse a capture. Legacy 5ug-gamma-poisson-v1 captures retain their eight-game prior, original forecasts and receipts, and are reproduced using that recorded configuration.
