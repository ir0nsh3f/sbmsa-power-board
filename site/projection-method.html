Retained result models

Completed flag-game details show the latest observed-public forecast before the exact season, sport, division, home team, away team and kickoff. Both capture/training cutoff and public observation must precede kickoff strictly. Equal observation instants choose the lexicographically greatest capture path. Rescheduled games do not inherit old forecasts; missing receipts or unknown kickoffs show “No archived pregame model.” Timestamp is the actual verified public-byte observation in Central time, not the exact initial publication time. Results are never used to refit this retained line. The compact verified results-model summary is rebuilt from validated immutable captures and receipts at publication, with source-check agreement enforced by the page. Archive or receipt damage blocks publication. This presentation does not change the evaluator: evaluation still selects the first observed-public pregame forecast. No cover/total accuracy metric is implied.

What the numbers mean

Home margin = projected home points minus projected away points. Positive means more home points; negative means more away points. Total = projected combined points. Scores, margins and totals are rounded to whole points independently, so rounded component scores can differ from their rounded total by one. Ranges are broad approximate 95% model-based predictive ranges, not empirically calibrated coverage or guarantees.

Model: flag-huber-ridge-v1

Each division is fitted independently, with no cross-division adjustment. Within a division, one score observation per team per completed game is modeled as:

score = division baseline + scoring team's offense + opponent's defense
minimize Σ Huber14(score − prediction)
         + 12/2 × (baseline − 21)²
         + 6/2 × Σ(offense² + defense²)

Defense is a points-allowed effect: lower is better. Neutral/shared grounds: home advantage is fixed at zero. Strong ridge regularization resolves sparse and disconnected schedules toward the same division baseline, not toward an inferred external division strength. No roster, injury, possession, weather or time-decay inputs. Outliers are not silently replaced with capped ranking margins; Huber loss has bounded score influence at residual ±14.

These are transparent defaults chosen for strong shrinkage and caution, not parameters learned through validated calibration. Unplayed teams in a division with data remain at prior effects and are labeled 0 GP. With no dated completed games in a division, no forecast is produced. Division games and both training-sample GP counts accompany each projection.

Uncertainty calculation

Residual SD is the greater of 14 and RMS fitted score residuals winsorized to ±28. A = X′WX + diagonal ridge weights from the last IRLS step. Parameter covariance is approximated by σ²A⁻¹. For home-minus-away or home-plus-away design vector v, predictive variance is σ²(2 + v′A⁻¹v). Displayed range = projected quantity ±1.96 standard deviations, endpoints rounded outward; total's lower endpoint is floored at zero. Expected scores are clamped to 0–70 before computing projected margin and total; realized margin/total ranges have no artificial upper cap.

This is a working ridge/normal approximation, not a validated robust posterior. Independent team-score noise is assumed; correlated game pace, discrete scoring, model misspecification and unknown team changes are not fully represented. Broad ranges can still miss. Early fits lean heavily on priors; do not treat small differences as established team separation.

Cutoffs and honest provenance

Only accepted official source collections are used. Collection time is recorded after all source fetches finish. Training uses numeric completed scores with a known timezone-aware scheduled start strictly before that collection cutoff. Unknown dates/times, future-dated results and partial scores do not enter training. Soccer receives no projections.

Forecasts require a future, unplayed, known kickoff strictly more than 30 minutes after collection; date-only games, already-started same-day fixtures and old missing results are withheld. Frontend comparisons use the actual start instant, not just the Central calendar date, and hide stale projections at kickoff. A forecast captured before kickoff is not automatically claimed to have been published before kickoff.

Archive and publication observations

  • projections/current.json: current estimates and calculation timestamp, training hash/cutoff, model/config, underlying capture path/time.
  • projections/index.json: append-only capture references and full-byte SHA-256 hashes. Each captures/<sha256>.json retains exact season/sport/division/home/away/kickoff identity, training scores, team list, version/config and predictions.
  • publication/<sha256>.json: separate immutable receipt written only after the deployed capture's bytes were fetched and verified online. Contains observed-public time and Actions run URL, not an invented exact publication time. A missing receipt means publication has not been verified. Receipts are committed after deployment and appear on the website in the next deployment.

Unchanged reruns do not create duplicate captures. Corrections, schedule changes and reversions append rather than rewrite; earlier forecasts remain intact. Collection/calculation timestamps on the current file may advance without changing its underlying capture. Archives validate before writes, and corruption stops publication rather than resetting history. An interrupted file/index transaction requires deliberate inspection and repair, not deletion.

Evaluation uses only the first capture of an exact fixture identity verified public strictly before kickoff. Later refits cannot replace that forecast. A moved kickoff creates a different identity; no automatic hindsight reassignment. Corrections to actual results can change evaluation residuals but never the stored forecast. The evaluation command is python scripts/projection_publication.py evaluate; no current-fit “backtest” is substituted when no eligible stored forecast exists.
