Opponent-adjusted power · Raw default

Method: opponent-ridge-margin-v3. Higher is stronger. Raw uses every point or goal of the actual final margin. There is no automatic blowout cap, Huber loss, or outlier downweighting. W–L–T is descriptive and never drives ordering or breaks power ties.

Exact objective

Default Raw: y_g = home_score − away_score
Optional cap: y_g = clip(home_score − away_score, −cap, +cap)
              cap = 21 points (flag), 3 goals (8U / 6U soccer)

Choose all team ratings r jointly to minimize:
  ½ Σ_g [y_g − (r_home − r_away)]² + (3/2) Σ_team r_team²

Home advantage = 0. Every completed game has weight 1.
Separate fit for each sport/age; no time decay or win-rate term.

A 40-point win contributes a 40-point target in Raw; a 20-point win contributes 20. A 14-goal win contributes 14, not 3. Ridge shrinks team ratings toward zero to stabilize small samples; it does not clip margins or downweight large residuals. Bigger margins have proportionately greater influence in Raw. The optional cap is applied only when you select it.

The zero-centered penalty is equivalent to three unit-weight pseudo-observations of each rating being zero. Under a Gaussian working model with residual variance σ², the independent rating-prior variance is σ²/3; no numerical σ or calibrated uncertainty is asserted. Strength 3 and optional caps are transparent editorial assumptions, not tuned forecasting claims. Early samples shrink more; additional games increasingly determine the fit.

Opponent adjustment is simultaneous: the normal equations give r_i = (sum of the team’s selected game margins + sum of opponents’ ratings)/(GP_i + 3). The same win against a stronger opponent earns more credit; the same loss to a weaker opponent costs more. This is a joint network fit, not a one-pass average of records.

Units and consistent views

A power rating is a points/goals strength effect, not the team’s average own-result margin. A rating difference is the fitted expected margin target between connected teams. This is not a calibrated betting spread, scoring projection or win probability. Do not compare sports or ages.

Raw is selected on every fresh page load. The cap toggle recalculates the main board, spotlight, Advanced board ranks, Our Teams opponent ranks, league matchup ranks and Top matchup eligibility together. Division/search filters preserve full-field rank identities. The descriptive Margin/G column follows the same mode; Advanced Cap Diff/G remains explicitly capped. Reproducibility

Pure JavaScript and standard-library Python start at zero, deterministically sort identities and games, and use simultaneous Jacobi updates. Stop at maximum rating change ≤10⁻¹²; fail rather than publish a partial solution after 10,000 iterations. Positive ridge makes the objective strictly convex and iteration a contraction. Tests check normal-equation residuals and numerical parity on fixtures and actual results, in both modes.

Rank by floor(power × 10⁸ + 0.5)/10⁸ descending. Equal values share competition ranks (1, 1, 3); team name then division supplies stable display order only. Display rounds to 2 decimals, so visually equal numbers can have distinct ranks. GP0 is unrated, not last; missing completed-game evidence cannot create a rating.

Disconnected schedules

Overall league-wide comparisons are provisional. Without games connecting result groups, scores cannot identify their relative strength. This occurs across divisions and even within a division early in the season. Ridge puts each group at zero mean by assumption; it does not calibrate division strength or prove groups are equally strong. Within connected groups, comparisons have result support but remain uncertain with small samples. The board reports result-group count.

Schedule Top matchup uses the selected mode’s full sport/age ranks: both teams at least 3 GP, both within ceil(all rated teams/4), with ties included. Rated 1–2 GP teams count in the denominator but cannot earn badges. Close records remains descriptive, never a close-game prediction.
