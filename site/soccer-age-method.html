Experimental 8U & 6U goal counts

Very small samples, broad uncertainty. Not sportsbook odds, shot-based xG, or calibrated predictions. These projections do not change power ranks or Watchlist.

Separate ages, all divisions

Fall 2026 models 8u-gamma-poisson-v1 and 6u-gamma-poisson-v1 use the approved three-prior-game Gamma–Poisson approach. 8U pools only Messi and Pulisic; 6U pools only Haaland, Messi and Mbappe. No observations from another age, 5U Girls, or flag enter either fit. Within each age, a shared scoring environment across divisions is an assumption—not measured equal division strength.

Mean goals

μ = scored goals divided by twice the number of numerically scored official games with a known aware kickoff before the actual accepted collection. At least four scored games and a positive μ are required. Paired outcome-only W/L results, partial scores, unknown kickoffs and future-dated scores do not train the model. Scored GP can differ from the official record’s GP.

For each team, attack a = (GF + 3μ)/(scored GP + 3), concession d = (GA + 3μ)/(scored GP + 3). Expected home goals = (home attack + away concession)/2; expected away goals = (away attack + home concession)/2. Three prior games shrink each rate. A zero-scored-game team starts at μ: this is prior extrapolation, not observed team strength.

No fitted strength-of-opponents adjustment, home advantage, roster, injury or time-decay effects. Early opponents and disconnected divisions can distort descriptive rates. This is not the separate opponent-adjusted Raw ridge power model.

Broad approximate ranges

Independent negative-binomial counts approximate Gamma-mixed Poisson goal rates. For each side, rate variance is one quarter of the two component posterior variances, (GF + 3μ)/(n + 3)² or (GA + 3μ)/(n + 3)², plus empirical baseline uncertainty and m²/2. Baseline uncertainty is max(μ + μ²/2, empirical population variance of team goals) divided by twice pooled scored games. Count variance also includes the Poisson mean m.

Deterministic convolution gives the 2.5% and 97.5% integer quantiles of home minus away and combined goals. Independence, dispersion shape 2, empirical baseline treatment and three-game shrinkage are assumptions, not calibrated coverage. Low scored GP can yield enormous uncertainty.

Lines and timing

The favorite is the team favored by the unrounded expected margin. Its displayed spread is minus the absolute margin rounded to the nearest half goal; rounded zero is Pick’em. Total is the combined expected goals independently rounded to a half goal. Do not derive the margin from separately rounded team goals.

Only wholly unplayed games with aware kickoff strictly more than 30 minutes after actual accepted collection are forecast. Unknown, partial, outcome-only final, already-started and past score-missing fixtures receive no new line. The browser hides lines at kickoff and rejects current payloads whose calculation timestamp differs from the accepted source check.

Immutable evidence, not retroactive history

Each age has its own append-only captures and exact-byte post-deployment observed-public receipts. Captures record model configuration, numeric training, source inputs, cutoff, generation time and exact season/sport/division/home/away/kickoff identity. Unchanged calculations can reuse an earlier capture. Generation or capture time is not proof of publication. Receipt observation is an upper bound on first publication, not an exact first-public time; receipts become public on the next deployment. No prior games are assigned invented pregame lines. Soccer has no last-pregame Results summary or validated predictive backtest yet.
